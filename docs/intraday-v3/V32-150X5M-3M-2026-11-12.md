# Market Forge DAY V3.2 Shadow: observed 150-symbol 5m candidate → retrospective completed 3m proxy
**Session:** Thursday 2026-11-12 (NYSE open), Mountain time. **Mode:** research only, no live 3m alerts, orders or MC/SCMP engine changes.

## Why
The 2026-11-11 IEX retrospective A/B showed 5 baseline 1+2+3m events vs 27 completed 3m-only events **within its hardcoded 20-symbol engine universe**. Only 2 of B passed as-of Top10+CHASE LOW. The historical 5m Top10 encountered 142 distinct symbols; hence running the existing V13.36 20-symbol engine against those 142 would be invalid. The next test must observe a larger 5m universe before claiming faster detection.

## Research experiment on Nov12
1. **Existing operational V3 150-symbol 5m Scanner** remains unchanged in its scoring, Top10 capacity, Telegram hourly/early WATCH and MC/SCMP position/exit routes. Date-gated logger writes **independent immutable rows only for Nov12**, in a separate transaction with errors explicitly caught. Events are emitted only when scanner actually observes a completed 5m decision, not backdated.
2. At **7:37 and 7:42 MT** provisional RADAR stores its new, as-observed tickers with `chase=UNKNOWN`; this **must not** be counted as validated CHASE LOW. The **7:47–7:57 EARLY WATCH** stores observed strong LOW candidates. From **8:02 MT** the main 150-symbol Scanner stores **all** 70+ / momentum 8+ / CHASE LOW eligible names, including those **outside** the hourly Top10. The logger records whether each was also selected in Top10.
3. After market close **14:33 MT (retry 14:40)**, a **separate** job reads the *real observed snapshots* and fetches Alpaca IEX 1Minute bars **for actual candidate symbols only** (from the 150-symbol universe). It builds a 3Minute candle **only from exactly three complete native 1m minutes**. No 1m interpolation; errors/stale/no data are counted instead of forced passes.
4. A **3m proxy** (candle body/close location, relative volume, VWAP/EMA9 trend, anti-extension, 5 consecutive full 3m candles) is evaluated **strictly AFTER** each 5m decision is recorded, and no later than 15 minutes afterward. Its name and numeric thresholds are **NOT the original 20-symbol V13.36 3m MC/SCMP engine**. It is a comparable research experiment, not a replacement signal or actual notification to the subscriber.
5. Log observation timestamps, confirmation delay, reason why unconfirmed, and signed +5/+15/+30 minutes subsequent *observed IEX prices*, only when the exact future 1m bar close exists. Do not report win rates, expected returns or fillable profits. Capture outcomes separately for OPEN_RADAR unknown chase, OPEN_EARLY LOW, 5M_STRONG_ALL LOW.
6. If no live 5m timeline, job **fails closed with no pretend report**. If the timeline exists with *zero eligible names*, report that as a real zero, not a data provider outage. If all fetched 1m bars are unusable, do not send a misleading success report. A one-per-day durable Telegram summary is sent after close only, with no new MC/SCMP intraday event alerts.
7. Read stored detailed data through the authenticated `/day-v32-shadow` screen. Job status visible in Vercel logs using `DAY_V32_SHADOW` and 5m receipt via `v32_capture` field. Auth uses existing webhook key. Never copy the key into chat.

## Rollout / safe boundaries
- Vercel cron: `33,40 20,21 * * 1-5`, local date/window **strictly Nov12 14:33–14:44 MT**. Wrong DST period and other dates no-op. No new Bigdata charges.
- Existing 7:35 Radar, 7:45 Early, 8:02 Top10, 5m new LOW, Overnight and Swing scanners stay exactly the same.
- The 11/11 V3.1 Freeze report is immutable. No modifications to its A/B, timelines, or Pine engine.

Promoting to real intraday 3m alerts requires observed timely IEX availability, forward shadow quality, options liquidity checks and a separately tested alert scheduler. A Vercel READY deployment alone does not confirm Cron ran, data existed or Telegram arrived.
